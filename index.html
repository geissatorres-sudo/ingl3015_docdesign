The CARP Principles
C

Contrast

If two elements aren't the same, make them very different. Contrast organizes information and guides the eye.

A

Alignment

Nothing on the page is placed arbitrarily. Every element connects visually to something else.

R

Repetition

Repeat visual elements — color, shape, font — throughout. Repetition builds unity and a sense of cohesion.

P

Proximity

Group related items together. Proximity creates organization and reduces clutter.
